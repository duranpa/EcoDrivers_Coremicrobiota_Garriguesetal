## EcoDrivers_Coremicrobiota_Garriguesetal
Scripts and data used for the Garrigues et al. paper

## Abstract

Abstract 
Plants and other photosynthetic organisms interact with their environment and surrounding microbiota through specialized associations. A global core microbiota has been proposed at high taxonomic levels, such as order level. However, it remains unclear whether this core microbiota varies at lower taxonomic resolution and whether environmental factors shape this variation. Here, we leveraged the ecological diversity of 141 sites across the southwest of France to characterize algal populations, and their associated bacterial and fungal microbiota. We then performed a meta-analysis, combining these data with published datasets to formally identify the global core microbiota of photosynthetic organisms, which comprises seven bacterial and five fungal orders. We next investigated diversity within this core microbiota and the environmental drivers shaping site-specific community composition. Thereby, we discovered that ASV-level core members are impacted by environmental factors, but that the core microbiota remains stable at order level. Using interaction network analysis, we finally explored how microbe-microbe interactions contribute to the assembly of stable core communities. Our results show that core taxa occupy central positions in algal-associated microbial networks and that distinct core members drive site-specific variation in core microbiota structure.
Together, these findings highlight the importance of both environmental context and microbial interactions in shaping the composition and stability of the core microbiota associated with photosynthetic organisms.


## Data and code availability

All processed data and code used to produce the manuscript’s figures are available at https://github.com/duranpa/EcoDrivers_Coremicrobiota_Garriguesetal. In addition, all raw reads are deposited at the ENA, under the accession PRJEB108334.
